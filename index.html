Our Story

A kitchen built on generations of flavour

Zaaika was born from a grandmother's recipe book and a chef's restless ambition. Founded by Chef Meera Pillai, our kitchen marries the ancient culinary wisdom of India — from the clay ovens of Rajasthan to the coconut coasts of Kerala — with seasonal, locally sourced ingredients.

We believe Indian cuisine is not a single story but a thousand distinct ones. Every dish we serve carries the memory of a region, a family, and a tradition passed down with love and exactitude.

Why Zaaika

The experience

🌶️

Spice Mastery

Every spice blend is ground fresh daily in our kitchen. Our masalas are crafted from whole spices sourced directly from farmers in Kerala, Rajasthan, and Tamil Nadu.

🏺

Clay & Copper

We cook in traditional clay pots and copper vessels, preserving the ancient alchemy that makes Indian food irreplaceable — slow-cooked flavours no modern pan can replicate.

🌿

Farm to Table

Our vegetables come from organic farms within 100 km of the restaurant. Our paneer is made in-house each morning, and our dairy is sourced from free-range Gir cows.
